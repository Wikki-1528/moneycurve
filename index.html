Common questions

Is MoneyCurve free?

Yes. Every tool is free and needs no account.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All math runs inside your browser. The site has no server that receives your numbers.

Is the CIBIL score real?

No. It is an estimate from four habits. Your real score comes from your credit report at the official CIBIL website.

How accurate are the results?

The formulas are standard, but returns and inflation are your assumptions. Banks may also add fees and taxes that are not included.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phone, tablet and desktop screens.
