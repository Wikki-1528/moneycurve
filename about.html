About MoneyCurve

A small set of money calculators built to give clear answers without sign-ups or ads.

What it is

MoneyCurve helps you answer everyday money questions: what a loan really costs, how much to invest for a goal, what a deposit will pay, and how prices eat into savings.

Privacy

The whole site is a set of static files. Every calculation runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server or saved.
